Mizan BLE

Pay in Mizan between two devices over Bluetooth LE.

One app, two modes: the host on an iPad enters an amount and advertises it; the client on an iPhone finds it, shows the amount, and confirms or declines.

  • v0.0.1 (built): both apps in the foreground, explicit handshake, mocked payment
  • v0.1.0: no explicit handshake
  • v0.2.0: no app in the foreground, if iOS allows it
