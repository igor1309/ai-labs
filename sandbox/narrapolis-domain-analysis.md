## Branding Analysis

### Etymology & Composition

- **Narra** — evokes "narrative", "narration", storytelling
- **Polis** — Greek for "city" (as in metropolis, cosmopolis)
- **Combined meaning**: "City of Stories" / "Narrative City"

### Strengths

| Factor | Rating | Notes |
|--------|--------|-------|
| Memorability | High | Distinctive, rolls off the tongue |
| Pronounceability | High | Clear syllables: na-RAP-o-lis |
| Spellability | Medium-High | Follows familiar "-polis" pattern |
| Uniqueness | Very High | No competing brands, all TLDs open |
| Emotional resonance | High | Evokes community, stories, place |
| Domain availability | Excellent | .com and all major TLDs available |

### Brand Positioning Opportunities

1. **Storytelling/Content platform** — the most natural fit. A place where stories live.
2. **Writing/Publishing tool** — city metaphor suggests a creative ecosystem, not just a tool.
3. **Interactive fiction/Games** — narrative-driven gaming community.
4. **Podcast/Audio network** — "city of narration" maps well to spoken word.
5. **Education/Learning** — stories as a teaching medium.

### Linguistic Qualities

- **Cadence**: 4 syllables with strong stress on the third (na-ra-PO-lis) — same rhythm as "Indianapolis", "Minneapolis"
- **Associations**: Feels established and real, like an actual city name — gives instant credibility
- **International**: Latin/Greek roots work across European languages; no negative meanings in major languages
- **Tone**: Intellectual but approachable, creative but grounded

### Risks & Considerations

- Could be misheard as "Narcopolis" (a film/book) — minor risk, context usually clarifies
- 10 characters — slightly long for a brand mark, but the "-polis" suffix is so recognizable it aids recall
- No obvious short form / nickname (could lean into "Narra" as informal shorthand)

### Recommended Domain Strategy

| Priority | Domain | Use Case |
|----------|--------|----------|
| 1 | narrapolis.com | Primary brand |
| 2 | narrapolis.app | Product/app |
| 3 | narrapolis.io | Tech/API |
| 4 | narrapolis.city | Marketing/landing page (thematic!) |

### Verdict

**Strong brand name.** The "-polis" suffix gives it gravitas and instant recognition as a "place", while "narra-" clearly signals storytelling. It's unique, available everywhere, and flexible enough to work across content, publishing, gaming, or education verticals. The .city TLD is a particularly nice thematic bonus.
