# Web Search and Company Research Strategies

## Company Intelligence Gathering

### Deep Company Research Queries

**Culture and Employee Sentiment:**
- `"[Company Name]" site:glassdoor.com reviews culture`
- `"[Company Name]" site:linkedin.com "life at" employee`
- `"[Company Name]" "work life balance" review -site:[company-domain]`
- `"[Company Name]" reddit honest opinion`
- `"[Company Name]" site:teamblind.com` (for tech companies)

**Leadership and Team Research:**
- `"[Hiring Manager Name]" site:linkedin.com background`
- `"[Company Name]" "[Department]" team structure`
- `"[CEO/CTO Name]" interview podcast transcript`
- `"[Company Name]" leadership changes announcement`

**Company Strategy and Direction:**
- `"[Company Name]" "annual report" strategy 2024 2025`
- `"[Company Name]" "earnings call transcript" priorities`
- `"[Company Name]" funding round investors tech press`
- `"[Company Name]" product roadmap announcement`

**News and Recent Developments:**
- `"[Company Name]" announcement last month site:techcrunch.com OR site:forbes.com`
- `"[Company Name]" layoffs hiring freeze expansion`
- `"[Company Name]" acquisition merger partnership`

### Finding Hidden Job Opportunities

**Direct Company Searches:**
- `site:[company-domain] careers "[Your Skill]" NOT "closed"`
- `"[Dream Company]" "we're hiring" "[Your Role]" -site:indeed.com -site:linkedin.com`
- `"[Company Name]" "join our team" "[Department]" email`

**Backdoor Opportunities:**
- `"[Company Name]" contractor vendor partner opportunities`
- `"[Company Name]" "[Your Skill]" consultant freelance`
- `"[Company Name]" internship pathway full-time`

## LinkedIn Intelligence

### Advanced LinkedIn Searches

**Finding the Right People:**
- Search for: "Hiring Manager" + "[Company Name]" + "[Department]"
- Look for: Recent posts about "building team" or "we're hiring"
- Filter by: "Posted in last month" + "People who work at [Company]"

**Identifying Champions:**
- Alumni from your school at target company
- People who share mutual connections
- Employees who frequently post about company culture
- People who've made similar career transitions

**Engagement Strategies:**
- Comment thoughtfully on their posts before reaching out
- Share their company's content with insightful commentary
- Join groups they're active in

### LinkedIn Message Templates for Cold Outreach

**For Alumni Connections:**
```
Hi [Name],

I noticed we're both [University] alumni - Go [Mascot]! I'm really interested in [Company]'s work in [specific project/area], especially [specific detail from research].

I saw you've been with the [Department] team for [timeframe]. I'd love to learn about your experience there and any advice you might have for someone looking to contribute to [specific company goal/project].

Would you be open to a brief coffee chat or phone call?
```

**For Skill-Based Connections:**
```
Hi [Name],

Your recent post about [specific topic] really resonated with me, especially your point about [specific detail]. 

I've been working in [related area] for [timeframe] and recently [relevant achievement]. I'm exploring opportunities at [Company] and would value your perspective on how the team approaches [specific challenge/area].

Could I ask you a few quick questions about your experience there?
```

## Creative Outreach Strategies

### Video Cover Letter Approach

**When to Use:**
- Creative roles or companies with creative culture
- When you have a unique story to tell
- To stand out in competitive situations
- When company values personality/culture fit

**Structure (90 seconds max):**
1. Hook (10 sec): Start with company-specific insight
2. Match (30 sec): Connect your experience to their needs
3. Unique value (30 sec): What differentiates you
4. Call to action (20 sec): Next steps

**Technical Tips:**
- Use Loom, Vidyard, or BombBomb for easy sharing
- Include captions for accessibility
- Keep background professional but personable
- Test audio quality before recording

### Social Media Targeting

**Twitter/X Strategy:**
- Follow company accounts and key employees
- Engage with their content meaningfully
- Share relevant industry insights and tag company thought leaders
- Use company hashtags when sharing relevant work

**Instagram/TikTok (for creative/consumer companies):**
- Create content showcasing relevant skills
- Use company products in creative ways
- Tag the company in relevant professional content
- Comment on company posts with valuable insights

### Portfolio/Project Targeting

**Create Company-Specific Work:**
- Redesign a page from their website
- Solve a public problem they're facing
- Create a feature spec for their product
- Write a blog post about their industry with actionable insights
- Build a demo using their API or technology

**Presentation Approach:**
- Create a 30/60/90 day plan for the role
- Design a "What I Would Do" deck
- Record a Loom walkthrough of your solution
- Share publicly and tag relevant employees

## Industry-Specific Research

### Tech Companies
- Check GitHub for open source contributions
- Look at their tech blog for stack and challenges
- Search Hacker News for employee comments
- Review their API documentation for product understanding

### Startups
- Search Crunchbase for funding and investor information
- Check AngelList for company culture and benefits
- Look for founder interviews on podcasts
- Research their investors' portfolio for context

### Enterprise/Corporate
- Read annual reports and SEC filings
- Check industry analyst reports (Gartner, Forrester)
- Review press releases for strategic initiatives
- Search for case studies featuring the company

## Competitive Intelligence

### Understanding the Landscape
- `"[Company Name]" vs OR versus OR competitor`
- `"[Company Name]" "market share" industry report`
- `"[Company Name]" strengths weaknesses analysis`
- `site:g2.com "[Company Product]" vs alternatives`

### Leveraging Competitive Knowledge
- Reference competitors respectfully in interviews
- Understand company's unique positioning
- Identify market gaps you could help address
- Show awareness of industry trends and challenges

## Timing Your Search

### Best Times to Apply
- Tuesday-Thursday (avoid Monday overwhelm, Friday distraction)
- Early morning (7-9 AM) in company's timezone
- Within 48 hours of job posting
- After engaging with company content on social media
- Following positive company news or funding announcements

### Following Up
- Wait 1 week after application before first follow-up
- Send follow-up to different person if no response
- Reference new company development in follow-up
- Add value with each touch point (share article, insight, etc.)
