# Traceability Review — Agonara Intent Layer (intent×intent)

- Cross-role findings: 0 fail, 0 warning.
- Inside-role findings: 0 fail, 0 warning.

Overall verdict: the intent layer is internally consistent — no factual contradiction or divergence was found across roles or within any single role file; two unresolved ambiguities are recorded as open questions rather than findings.

## Cross-role findings

No contradictions found.

The role boundaries are partitioned deliberately and reinforce each other rather than collide. The corpus/student-state separation asserted in the product intent (`intent_learning_program_app.md:135`, `:162`, `:166`) is upheld by every role file: the curator boundary repeats "Program corpus and student state must remain separate." (`curator_user_stories.md:181`), the student boundary repeats it (`student_user_stories.md:226`), and student stories that touch progress explicitly disclaim mutating the corpus (e.g. STU-08 `student_user_stories.md:88`). Membership authority is likewise partitioned without overlap: curator and student perform normal membership operations (CUR-14..CUR-18, STU-18..STU-23) while the admin file scopes its membership stories to "exceptional recovery and audit operations, not the normal product flow" (ADM boundary, `admin_user_stories.md:189`) and ADM-08 guarantees admin changes "do not automatically grant Student or Program Curator authority" (`admin_user_stories.md:105`). Evidence preservation on membership change is consistent across CUR-17 (`curator_user_stories.md:163`), STU-19 (`student_user_stories.md:184`), and ADM-14 (`admin_user_stories.md:169`). The remaining language difference — Russian product-intent prose vs. English story bodies — is not a semantic contradiction and is not flagged.

## Open questions

These are ambiguities the intent layer alone does not resolve. They are candidates for a human resolution session, not findings.

1. Membership "operation" vs "policy" for curators. The curator boundary states curators "do not define membership policy, invitation policy, or membership recovery" (`curator_user_stories.md:179`), yet CUR-14 (invite, `:139`) and CUR-17 (remove, `:163`) are membership-changing operations. This is internally defensible if "performing a membership operation" is distinct from "defining membership policy," but the intent layer never states that distinction explicitly, so the line between a curator's normal operation and the policy/recovery space reserved elsewhere is left implicit.
2. Program type vs. join mode. STU-18 targets a "self-join program" (`student_user_stories.md:172`) while STU-22 accepts an invitation to a "curator-controlled program" (`student_user_stories.md:204`). The intent layer does not state whether self-join and invitation are mutually exclusive program types, two policy modes a single program can expose, or overlapping. No story contradicts another here, but the join-mode taxonomy is unspecified — the product intent itself lists "Где проходит продуктовая граница…" and the membership policy questions as open (`intent_learning_program_app.md:170-175`).
