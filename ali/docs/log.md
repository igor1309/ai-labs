## 2026-10-22 — Registration-stage application and initial concept

- Began exploring participation in the [Create AI Agents Championship](https://www.dubaichamberdigital.com/en/create-ai-agents).
- Chose the **societal impact → education → personalized learning** direction, aimed at **learners of all ages**.
- Combined three proposed capabilities: **Autonomous Learning Coach**, **Adaptive Teaching Intelligence**, and **Personal Learning Twin**.
- Preferred **collaborative + adaptive autonomy** and an **integrated cognitive, behavioral, and metacognitive Learning Twin**.
- Selected a **subject-agnostic vision with a narrowly focused prototype**. The prototype subject and primary differentiating innovation remained open.
- Explicitly ruled out **mathematics, English language, and programming** as first prototype subjects; left other learning domains for future evaluation.
- Declined further premature design decisions and shifted focus to completing the registration form.
- Four form-answer drafts were prepared, respecting the specified maximum lengths:
  1. How the agent would solve the problem — 150-word maximum.
  2. What would differentiate it from existing solutions — 150-word maximum.
  3. How it could create value in the future — 150-word maximum.
  4. Why participate in the championship — 75-word maximum.
- **Full verbatim texts:** [application.md](application.md).
