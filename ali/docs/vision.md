# Vision: A Lifelong Learning Companion

**Status:** Exploratory vision, subject to research, prototyping, and evaluation.

## North star

An AI learning companion that evolves with each learner over time, making useful, personalized education more accessible while strengthening the learner's ability to learn independently.

## Proposed experience

A learner sets an objective and can revise it. The agent collaborates on a learning journey, estimates current understanding, suggests an approach, teaches or facilitates practice, checks for understanding, and updates its model using observed evidence. Over time, accumulated context could help the agent select more effective strategies and support transitions across subjects and life stages.

## Design pillars

### 1. Continuity: Personal Learning Twin

A persistent learner model combining cognitive, behavioral, and metacognitive signals. It should distinguish observations, hypotheses, and uncertain inferences, and give learners meaningful visibility and control over their information.

### 2. Agency: Autonomous Learning Coach

Within agreed boundaries, the agent could propose learning plans, identify knowledge gaps, adjust sequencing, and recommend next steps. Learners would retain influence over goals and levels of autonomy.

### 3. Adaptation: Teaching Intelligence

The agent could test different teaching and practice methods, respond to evidence of understanding, and revise approaches when progress stalls. Claims of superior pedagogy would require evaluation.

### 4. Independence: Learning How to Learn

An important ambition is to improve self-assessment, reflection, reasoning, and independent learning, so that the learner increasingly directs their own development.

### 5. Reach: Accessibility and lifelong learning

The long-term ambition spans multiple ages, learning contexts, and subjects. Early validation should focus on a narrowly defined learner group and outcome before widening scope.

## Long-term value hypotheses

- More accessible tailored learning support.
- Better understanding of personal learning gaps and misconceptions.
- Improved learning outcomes and independent learning strategies.
- Support for educational transitions, reskilling, and lifelong education.

These are hypotheses to test; no outcomes have yet been measured.

## Decisions deliberately left open

- Product name ("ALI" is a working label only).
- Initial subject, curriculum, and learner cohort.
- Primary unique selling proposition.
- Technical architecture, model choices, interfaces, and deployment.
- Evaluation methodology and claims about educational effectiveness.

## Near-term principle

Use the championship's workshops, community, and forthcoming submission guidance to turn this broad vision into a credible, testable first use case. Preserve flexibility until evidence supports narrower decisions.
