# Concept: Personalized AI Learning Agent

**Status:** Exploratory proposal, recorded 2026-10-22.  
**Working name:** ALI (Adaptive Learning Intelligence), provisional only.  
**Proposed competition category:** Best Societal AI Agent.  
**Domain:** Education / personalized learning.  
**Audience:** Learners of all ages.

## Problem and opportunity

Learning needs differ across people, subjects, and life stages. A universal, personalized learning companion could help learners understand what they know, identify gaps, find useful ways to learn, and develop greater independence. Accessibility and educational equity are intended benefits to investigate, rather than outcomes already demonstrated.

## Proposed approach

The envisioned agent would:

1. Work with learners to define learning goals.
2. Assess understanding and identify potential knowledge gaps and misconceptions.
3. Develop and revise a personalized learning plan.
4. Select and adapt explanations, activities, and assessment approaches using observed progress.
5. Maintain a persistent, evolving **Personal Learning Twin** across learning interactions.
6. Help learners strengthen self-assessment, critical thinking, and independent learning strategies.

These are intended capabilities, not validated implementation claims.

## Three core capabilities selected for exploration

| Capability | Proposed function |
| --- | --- |
| **Autonomous Learning Coach** | Assess knowledge, plan learning activities, track progress, and suggest adjustments. |
| **Adaptive Teaching Intelligence** | Vary pedagogy, level, and exercises in response to demonstrated understanding. |
| **Personal Learning Twin** | Maintain a longitudinal record/model of understanding, misconceptions, habits, and learning development. |

## Learning Twin dimensions

- **Cognitive:** Knowledge, skills, misconceptions, retention, and mastery.
- **Behavioral:** Study patterns, engagement, persistence, and preferences, using appropriate and consented evidence.
- **Metacognitive:** Self-assessment, confidence calibration, choice of learning strategies, and reflection.

These dimensions represent design hypotheses. How to measure them reliably and responsibly remains to be determined.

## Autonomy model

**Collaborative + adaptive autonomy** was selected as an exploration direction:

- Learners define or approve their goals and remain able to intervene.
- The agent may propose and manage plans, teaching approaches, and progress assessments within learner-approved boundaries.
- The degree of initiative should adjust with learner age, competence, preferences, and independence; additional safeguards would be required for minors.

## Product scope and prototype

- **Long-term design direction:** A subject-agnostic architecture potentially usable in academic, professional, and general education.
- **Prototype strategy:** Validate a narrower educational use case first.
- **Prototype subject:** Open. Mathematics, English language, and programming were explicitly rejected as initial subject choices; critical thinking, foundational literacy, scientific reasoning, and learning how to learn remained open.
- **Differentiating innovation:** Open. The Personal Learning Twin and autonomous pedagogy are possible points of differentiation, not a validated novelty claim.

## Open questions

- Which narrow learning problem offers a credible first prototype and measurable outcomes?
- What evidence will support knowledge and mastery estimates?
- How will effectiveness be measured against existing tools or instruction?
- What learner data, consent, privacy safeguards, and age-appropriate controls are necessary?
- Which components genuinely require autonomous agent behavior?
