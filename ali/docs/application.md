## 1. How do you imagine your AI agent solving this problem?

**Form limit:** 150 words

I envision an autonomous AI learning companion that makes personalized education accessible to people of all ages and backgrounds.

The agent would develop a continuously evolving understanding of each learner's knowledge, skills, misconceptions, learning habits, and progress — creating a dynamic personal learning model.

Based on this understanding, it would identify knowledge gaps, recommend learning paths, adapt teaching strategies, and continuously assess understanding. Rather than following a fixed curriculum, the agent would collaborate with learners to define their goals and dynamically adjust its approach based on their needs and demonstrated progress.

A key ambition is to help people become better independent learners by developing their critical thinking, self-assessment, and learning strategies.

Ultimately, the goal is to create an intelligent learning companion that evolves alongside each individual, making effective, personalized, lifelong learning more accessible.

## 2. What makes your AI agent idea different from existing solutions?

**Form limit:** 150 words

The key differentiator is the concept of a persistent, evolving Personal Learning Twin that enables the AI agent to understand not only what a person knows, but also how they learn, think, and develop over time.

Unlike conventional AI tutors that primarily respond to questions or adapt individual lessons, this agent would build a longitudinal understanding of each learner, integrating cognitive, behavioral, and metacognitive insights.

This would enable the agent to autonomously identify learning needs, experiment with teaching strategies, evaluate their effectiveness, and continuously refine its approach in collaboration with the learner.

Another distinctive ambition is to develop learning independence rather than dependence on AI. The agent would progressively help learners strengthen their self-awareness, critical thinking, and ability to direct their own education.

The vision is a lifelong learning companion that evolves with the individual across subjects, educational stages, and changing personal goals.

## 3. How do you imagine your AI agent creating value in the future?

**Form limit:** 150 words

I envision this AI agent creating long-term societal value by making high-quality, personalized education more accessible, effective, and affordable for people of all ages and backgrounds.

By adapting to individual learning needs, it could help reduce educational inequalities, improve learning outcomes, and support people who struggle with conventional teaching approaches.

Beyond acquiring knowledge, the agent would help individuals develop critical thinking, self-awareness, and independent learning skills — capabilities increasingly essential in a rapidly changing world.

Over time, it could support lifelong learning, professional transitions, and continuous personal development, helping people adapt to technological and economic changes.

Its potential extends to educational institutions, community organizations, and underserved populations, where personalized learning support is often limited.

Ultimately, the greatest value would come from empowering individuals to take ownership of their education and develop the confidence and capabilities to keep learning throughout their lives.

## 4. Why should you join the Create AI Agents Championship?

**Form limit:** 75 words

I want to join the Create AI Agents Championship to turn an ambitious vision for personalized, lifelong learning into a practical, impactful AI solution. The championship offers an opportunity to challenge my assumptions, learn from experts, collaborate with innovators, and validate the concept through real-world experimentation. I hope to develop an AI agent that demonstrates how autonomous intelligence can make education more accessible, adaptive, and empowering for everyone.
