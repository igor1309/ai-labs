# Jaw picture credits

The Whose Jaw? pictures: each dinosaur's loose jaw, `<id>.webp` here (600×450 WebP with alpha), and its gummy mouth, `../dinos/<id>/gummy.webp` (800×600 WebP with alpha, edited from its own `body.webp`). Both are made by `scripts/jaw-picture.sh` to the jaw and gummy mouth prompts in `art/style.md` and the jaw lines in `art/prompts.md`. All are AI-generated for RexLab; no third-party artwork or photos are used. Facts are in `src/data/jaws.json`.
