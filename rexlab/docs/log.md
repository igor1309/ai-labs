# rexlab — Change Log

## 2026-10-17

- Moved dinosaur care's designs from `design/care.pen` into `design/rexlab.pen`, the one design file, and deleted `care.pen`: the welcome screen, the visits, the result, the friends list and its empty state, the dark visit and the item art, under the sorting game's frames. Its home frame is left out, since the sorting game's home frame shows the same three columns with the CARE tile.
- Gave the sorting game its result and progress (LAB-272), not yet reachable from the home page: a finished board shows the score in six stars, counting up, with the best score, PLAY AGAIN for a board sorting by another property, and HOME; a first best or a new best is celebrated with confetti. The best score and finished rounds are kept under `sort-dinosaurs` in the shared progress record, beside the quiz's and care's. The three games now share one best-score rule and the result screen's chips and count-up.
- Built the sorting game's board (LAB-272), not yet reachable from the home page: six dinosaur cards on a tray over two baskets, sorting by legs, food, size, horns or feathers, never the same property twice in a row, with at least two cards for each basket and only dinosaurs whose fact is clear. A card is dragged or tapped and then its basket tapped; the right basket takes it as a small card with a ✓. A wrong basket sends it back to the tray with its right basket's symbol, and the right basket glows. Every card, basket, landing and hint animates with the shared motion tokens, and with reduced motion each is a short fade. The traits are read and checked at start-up, and the game lives in `src/sorting/` behind its own entry point.
- Added dinosaur care (LAB-271): a CARE tile under ALL DINOSAURS, in place of the locked food game, opens a welcome screen with NEW VISITOR and FRIENDS. A random dinosaur from the shared list visits, never the last one, and asks in a picture bubble to be fed, given water, bathed and put to rest, in that order. The hungry bubble is an empty bowl for every dinosaur, so the child picks the food from what they know about its diet. A suitable item earns two hearts, or one after a refusal. Any other item gets a gentle turn of the head, and then the bubble shows what would do and an amber ring marks it. Taps during a reaction count for nothing. Once rested, the dinosaur dances off to a result of 4 to 8 hearts, with first-best and new-best celebrations, and becomes a friend who can be called back from FRIENDS. Care progress (the best score, finished visits and friends) is kept in the versioned record beside the quiz's, recorded the moment the last need is met. The item pictures are 400×300 WebPs with alpha, cut tight to their art and made with pen.dev AI image generation, in `src/assets/care/`, credited there. Every arrival, reaction, heart and departure animates with the shared motion tokens, and with reduced motion each is a short fade. Two care scenarios now give the hearts a child can actually have at that need. `AGENTS.md` now says a game is built one screen per implementer step, then one step for the full flow. The home page is now in three columns, 3 : 2 : 1 of its width: COMPARE, then ALL DINOSAURS over CARE, then a MORE COMING card as tall as them, a faded Triceratops over a lock, a picture rather than a button, as the locked game was. The tiles' titles stay at least 24px on one line on every iPad, with their counts, at least 20px, in the pictures' corners, as COMPARE's best score sits in its card's corner. Care's API now speaks only of visits, needs and items, and the visit screen's view, its bubble with the empty bowl and its suggestions, is made where the app presents it; tests now tie every visitor's diet to its record and keep a picture that loads after the child has gone home from starting a visit.
- Designed dinosaur care (LAB-271), now in `design/rexlab.pen`: the home page with ALL DINOSAURS, SORT and CARE stacked beside COMPARE, the welcome screen, a visit with its four-need progress, hearts, a thought bubble and five picture buttons, a plant-eater turning meat away while PLANTS is suggested, two hearts popping up, the result with a new best and a new friend, the friends list and its empty state, and a dark visit. The item pictures (plants, meat, water, bath, nest and an empty bowl) are generated in the dinosaurs' picture-book style, in `design/images/care-*.webp`.
- Specified dinosaur care in seven `@next` features (LAB-278): welcoming a visitor, meeting four pictured needs using the shared MEAT / PLANTS / BOTH diets, friendly retries and hearts, best scores, saved friends and recall, storage failures, and EN/RU, theme and motion behaviour. The proposed score is 4–8 hearts per visit: two for a first-choice match, one after retrying. The hungry bubble shows an empty bowl, never the food itself, so the child chooses the food from what they know about the dinosaur; after a refusal the bubble shows the right food. The CARE tile is the last of the tiles beside COMPARE, under ALL DINOSAURS and SORT, as the owner ruled, and shows the care best score. Design and implementation follow separately.
- Added the dinosaur catalogue (LAB-268): an ALL DINOSAURS tile on the home page in place of the locked TALLER? game opens a list of all 30 dinosaurs, four to a row, each card with its height, weight and a MEAT, PLANTS or BOTH food tag in colour, symbol and word. A card opens the dinosaur's page: a big picture, the food tag under the name, a short description in capitals, height and weight, and a small note for parents naming the sources; ← and → go round the whole list. Every dinosaur in `dinosaurs.json` now has its food and its description in English and Russian, and the app stops at start-up if one is missing. The picture flies from the card to the page and back, pages slide in from the side once their picture is decoded (waiting at most a second) while the neighbours load ahead, and the cards rise in one after the other; with reduced motion all of it is a short fade. Specified in `docs/features/catalogue.feature`.
- Made every change on screen smooth (LAB-277): screens cross-fade with a slight lift, the next question's pair slides in from the right once its pictures are decoded (waiting at most a second), an answer squishes the tapped card, stamps its ✓ or ✗ and raises the values one after the other before NEXT slides up, the progress step grows, the result counts its score up and pops its stars in one by one under drifting confetti, and the parent menus grow from their button. The easing and durations are shared motion tokens in `app.css`; with reduced motion everything is a short fade. `AGENTS.md` now makes motion a product principle: an abrupt change is a defect.
- Specified that every question, the first of a round too, waits up to a second for its pictures, so no picture pops in, and that a double tap on COMPARE starts one round (LAB-277).
- Added 12 more dinosaurs, from 18 to 30 (LAB-269): Carnotaurus, Giganotosaurus, Therizinosaurus, Edmontosaurus, Deinonychus, Oviraptor, Coelophysis, Psittacosaurus, Styracosaurus, Kentrosaurus, Baryonyx and Apatosaurus, with height and mass from the trusted sources and artwork in the same picture-book style; Deinonychus, Oviraptor and Therizinosaurus are feathered.
- Designed the dinosaur catalogue in `design/rexlab.pen` (LAB-268): a list of all dinosaurs with height, weight and a coloured MEAT or PLANTS tag on each card, a page per dinosaur with its food type as a tag under the name, and a catalogue tile for the home page.
