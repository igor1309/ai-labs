# rexlab — Change Log

## 2026-10-17

- Added find the pair (LAB-280): a FIND THE PAIR tile in place of MORE COMING, two cards of a Stegosaurus beside a face-down card, its title under its symbol on two lines at most, opens a welcome screen with PLAY, the level and the stars. Each level deals pairs of different dinosaurs from the shared list, never one of the board before: 3 pairs in 2×3, 4 in 2×4, then 4 and 6 pairs where a picture matches its silhouette, with no look-alikes. Two cards are open at most; a match stays open with a bounce, a green border and its name, sized from the card's width so the longest name fits, and a miss turns back after a second, with no cross and no wrong word. There is no timer and no move limit. The last pair makes every card wave before the result: the tries, a star, first-best and new-best celebrations of the level's fewest tries, a level up after two boards with a peek at the next board, and the board's dinosaurs, NEW for each found for the first time. Found dinosaurs are stamped in ALL DINOSAURS and on their pages, with an "N / 42" count. Progress (stars, the level, each level's best and the dinosaurs found) is kept in the versioned record beside the other games', recorded the moment the last pair is found. A board waits up to a second for its pictures, every turn, bounce and wave animates with the shared motion tokens, and with reduced motion each is a short fade.
- Designed find the pair in `design/rexlab.pen` (LAB-280): the home page with a FIND THE PAIR tile in place of MORE COMING, the welcome screen with PLAY, the level and the stars, one board per level (3 pairs in 2×3, 4 in 2×4, 4 picture-to-silhouette in 2×4, 6 picture-to-silhouette in 3×4) showing a found pair, a miss, a silhouette match and the dark theme, the result with the tries, a new best, a level up with the next board and NEW dinosaurs, and ALL DINOSAURS and a dinosaur page with found stamps and a "6 / 42" count. Two frames check that the 3×4 board fits at 1180×820 with 180px cards and at 1133×744 with the minimum 150px cards. The silhouettes are made from the pictures in `design/images/silhouette-*.webp`. The specs now give the home tile's place and the locked board sizes.

- Specified a featured game for the home page in three `@next` features, `home-featured-*.feature` (LAB-286): the large card on the left features one game a day, an extra door beside its tile, which never moves, so every game keeps its place whichever is featured. The choice is random in tile order, with a weight of 2 for the games started least (ties share it) and 1 for the others, so the same game may come up two days running. A day is a calendar day on the device's clock, and the choice never changes while the app is open. Each game names what counts as a start, an unfinished one included; browsing inside a game does not count. The featured card shows its tile's best score or count, the tiles scroll sideways once they no longer fit, in both languages, and the choice and start counts are kept beside other progress, which each side leaves alone, best effort. Design and implementation follow separately.

- Added dinosaur care (LAB-271): a CARE tile under ALL DINOSAURS, in place of the locked food game, opens a welcome screen with NEW VISITOR and FRIENDS. A random dinosaur from the shared list visits, never the last one, and asks in a picture bubble to be fed, given water, bathed and put to rest, in that order. The hungry bubble is an empty bowl for every dinosaur, so the child picks the food from what they know about its diet. A suitable item earns two hearts, or one after a refusal. Any other item gets a gentle turn of the head, and then the bubble shows what would do and an amber ring marks it. Taps during a reaction count for nothing. Once rested, the dinosaur dances off to a result of 4 to 8 hearts, with first-best and new-best celebrations, and becomes a friend who can be called back from FRIENDS. Care progress (the best score, finished visits and friends) is kept in the versioned record beside the quiz's, recorded the moment the last need is met. The item pictures are 400×300 WebPs with alpha, cut tight to their art and made with pen.dev AI image generation, in `src/assets/care/`, credited there. Every arrival, reaction, heart and departure animates with the shared motion tokens, and with reduced motion each is a short fade. Two care scenarios now give the hearts a child can actually have at that need. `AGENTS.md` now says a game is built one screen per implementer step, then one step for the full flow. The home page is now in three columns, 3 : 2 : 1 of its width: COMPARE, then ALL DINOSAURS over CARE, then a MORE COMING card as tall as them, a faded Triceratops over a lock, a picture rather than a button, as the locked game was. The tiles' titles stay at least 24px on one line on every iPad, with their counts, at least 20px, in the pictures' corners, as COMPARE's best score sits in its card's corner. Care's API now speaks only of visits, needs and items, and the visit screen's view, its bubble with the empty bowl and its suggestions, is made where the app presents it; tests now tie every visitor's diet to its record and keep a picture that loads after the child has gone home from starting a visit.
