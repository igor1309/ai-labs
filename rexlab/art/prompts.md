# Dinosaur prompts

Add each line to the style prompt in `style.md`.

- `tyrannosaurus`: Tyrannosaurus, large head, tiny two-fingered arms, thick tail, brown and olive.
- `triceratops`: Triceratops, three horns and a wide bony frill, four sturdy legs, green and tan.
- `velociraptor`: Velociraptor, small and slim, covered in feathers, long stiff tail, sickle claw on each foot, rust and cream.
- `brachiosaurus`: Brachiosaurus, very long neck held upright, front legs longer than back legs, small head, grey-blue.
- `stegosaurus`: Stegosaurus, two rows of tall back plates, four tail spikes, small head, green with orange plates.
- `ankylosaurus`: Ankylosaurus, low and wide, armoured back with knobs, club at the tail tip, sandy brown.
- `spinosaurus`: Spinosaurus, long crocodile-like snout, tall sail on its back, teal and orange.
- `diplodocus`: Diplodocus, very long neck and whip-like tail held level, small head, grey-green.
- `parasaurolophus`: Parasaurolophus, long curved tube crest on the head, duck-like bill, yellow with red stripes.
- `pachycephalosaurus`: Pachycephalosaurus, thick domed head ringed with small knobs, walks on two legs, tan and purple.
- `iguanodon`: Iguanodon, thumb spikes on its hands, beak-like mouth, green and grey.
- `compsognathus`: Compsognathus, tiny and slender, long neck and tail, walks on two legs, light green.
- `gallimimus`: Gallimimus, ostrich-like, long legs, small head with a beak, light brown.
- `allosaurus`: Allosaurus, big head with small horns above the eyes, three-fingered arms, orange-brown.
- `argentinosaurus`: Argentinosaurus, enormous sauropod, long neck and tail, thick pillar legs, earthy grey.
- `microraptor`: Microraptor, tiny, feathered wings on both arms and legs, glossy black-blue feathers.
- `protoceratops`: Protoceratops, small, parrot-like beak, short frill, no big horns, sandy yellow.
- `dilophosaurus`: Dilophosaurus, two thin crests on top of its head, slim, walks on two legs, green with yellow.
