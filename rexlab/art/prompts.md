# Dinosaur prompts

Add each line to the style prompt in `style.md`.

- `tyrannosaurus`: Tyrannosaurus, large head, tiny two-fingered arms, thick tail, brown and olive.
- `triceratops`: Triceratops, three horns and a wide bony frill, four sturdy legs, green and tan.
- `velociraptor`: Velociraptor, small and slim, covered in feathers, long stiff tail, sickle claw on each foot, rust and cream.
- `brachiosaurus`: Brachiosaurus, very long neck held upright, front legs longer than back legs, small head, grey-blue.
- `stegosaurus`: Stegosaurus, two rows of tall back plates, four tail spikes, small head, green with orange plates.
- `ankylosaurus`: Ankylosaurus, low and wide, armoured back with knobs, club at the tail tip, sandy brown.
- `spinosaurus`: Spinosaurus, long crocodile-like snout, tall sail on its back, teal and orange.
- `diplodocus`: Diplodocus, very long neck and whip-like tail held level, small head, grey-green.
- `parasaurolophus`: Parasaurolophus, long curved tube crest on the head, duck-like bill, yellow with red stripes.
- `pachycephalosaurus`: Pachycephalosaurus, thick domed head ringed with small knobs, walks on two legs, tan and purple.
- `iguanodon`: Iguanodon, thumb spikes on its hands, beak-like mouth, green and grey.
- `compsognathus`: Compsognathus, tiny and slender, long neck and tail, walks on two legs, light green.
- `gallimimus`: Gallimimus, ostrich-like, long legs, small head with a beak, light brown.
- `allosaurus`: Allosaurus, big head with small horns above the eyes, three-fingered arms, orange-brown.
- `argentinosaurus`: Argentinosaurus, enormous sauropod, long neck and tail, thick pillar legs, earthy grey.
- `microraptor`: Microraptor, tiny, feathered wings on both arms and legs, glossy black-blue feathers.
- `protoceratops`: Protoceratops, small, parrot-like beak, short frill, no big horns, sandy yellow.
- `dilophosaurus`: Dilophosaurus, two thin crests on top of its head, slim, walks on two legs, green with yellow.
- `carnotaurus`: Carnotaurus, short deep head with two thick horns above the eyes, tiny stubby arms, long legs, red-brown.
- `giganotosaurus`: Giganotosaurus, huge long head with a low ridge on the snout, three-fingered arms, thick tail, grey-brown with dark stripes.
- `therizinosaurus`: Therizinosaurus, pot-bellied, long neck and small head, very long claws on its hands, covered in fluffy feathers, cream and rust.
- `edmontosaurus`: Edmontosaurus, wide flat duck-like bill, no crest, long tail, stands on all fours or two legs, olive and cream.
- `deinonychus`: Deinonychus, slim and fast, covered in feathers, long stiff tail, big sickle claw on each foot, dark grey with white.
- `oviraptor`: Oviraptor, short parrot-like beak, rounded crest on the head, covered in feathers, walks on two legs, blue-grey and orange.
- `coelophysis`: Coelophysis, very slim, long narrow head, long neck and tail, walks on two legs, yellow-green with stripes.
- `psittacosaurus`: Psittacosaurus, small, parrot-like beak, little cheek horns, a row of bristles on its tail, walks on two legs, tan and brown.
- `styracosaurus`: Styracosaurus, one long nose horn and long spikes around the edge of its frill, four sturdy legs, brown and gold.
- `kentrosaurus`: Kentrosaurus, small plates on the neck turning into long spikes along the back and tail, a spike on each shoulder, ochre and dark green.
- `baryonyx`: Baryonyx, long narrow crocodile-like snout, big claw on each thumb, walks on two legs, green-brown.
- `apatosaurus`: Apatosaurus, very long thick neck held low, long whip tail, bulky body, pillar legs, warm grey.
- `ceratosaurus`: Ceratosaurus, a short horn on its nose and small horns above the eyes, a row of little bumps down its back, walks on two legs, orange and dark brown stripes.
- `utahraptor`: Utahraptor, big and strong, covered in feathers, long stiff tail, huge sickle claw on each foot, brown with white speckles.
- `yutyrannus`: Yutyrannus, big two-legged hunter covered in long fluffy feathers, a low crest along its snout, small three-fingered arms, white and grey.
- `archaeopteryx`: Archaeopteryx, small, feathered wings with three clawed fingers, long feathered tail, small teeth, walks on two legs, black and brown feathers.
- `troodon`: Troodon, slim, covered in feathers, very big round eyes, long legs and a long tail, walks on two legs, sand and grey feathers.
- `heterodontosaurus`: Heterodontosaurus, tiny, short beak with two small tusks, long hands, long tail, walks on two legs, green and tan.
- `mamenchisaurus`: Mamenchisaurus, extremely long neck held straight out forward, level with its back and not raised, small head, long tail, pillar legs, light brown with darker bands.
- `amargasaurus`: Amargasaurus, small sauropod, two rows of tall thin spines along its neck and back, four legs, long tail, red-brown and cream.
- `plateosaurus`: Plateosaurus, long neck and small head, walks on two strong back legs, short arms with big thumb claws, long tail, olive green and yellow.
- `maiasaura`: Maiasaura, duck-like bill, small bony bump above the eyes, stands on all fours, long tail, blue-green and cream.
- `corythosaurus`: Corythosaurus, tall round crest on the head like a helmet, duck-like bill, stands on all fours, red and gold.
- `chasmosaurus`: Chasmosaurus, very wide and long solid frill covered in skin, no holes in the frill, short horns, four sturdy legs, purple-grey with a pink frill.

## Flying and sea reptiles

These are not dinosaurs: add each line to the flying or sea style prompt
in `style.md` in place of the dinosaur one.

- `quetzalcoatlus`: Quetzalcoatlus, a giant pterosaur, very long stiff neck, huge long pointed toothless beak, small rounded crest, long narrow wings spread wide, fine short fuzz on its body, pale grey and teal with an orange beak.
- `pteranodon`: Pteranodon, a pterosaur, long toothless beak, long crest sweeping back from the head, long wings spread wide, short tail, fine short fuzz on its body, cream and slate blue with a red-orange crest.
- `rhamphorhynchus`: Rhamphorhynchus, a small pterosaur, long narrow jaws with sharp teeth pointing forward, long thin tail ending in a small diamond-shaped vane, narrow wings spread, fine short fuzz on its body, olive green and yellow.
- `dimorphodon`: Dimorphodon, a small pterosaur, big deep head like a puffin's with small teeth, short wings spread, long thin tail, fine short fuzz on its body, black and bright yellow.
- `pterodactylus`: Pterodactylus, a small pterosaur, long slim beak with small teeth, small low crest, very short tail, wings spread, fine short fuzz on its body, brown and tan.
- `mosasaurus`: Mosasaurus, a giant sea lizard, long body, big head with long jaws, four paddle flippers, a tail ending in a fin that bends down, blue-grey back and pale belly.
- `elasmosaurus`: Elasmosaurus, a plesiosaur, extremely long neck held straight out forward, small head, round body, four long flippers, short tail, sea green and cream.
- `kronosaurus`: Kronosaurus, a pliosaur, huge long head with big jaws, short thick neck, heavy body, four big flippers, short tail, brown and sand.
- `plesiosaurus`: Plesiosaurus, a plesiosaur, long neck, small head, broad flat body, four flippers, short tail, orange-brown with a cream belly.
- `ophthalmosaurus`: Ophthalmosaurus, an ichthyosaur, dolphin-shaped body, very big round eyes, long thin snout, a fin on its back, a half-moon tail fin, four flippers, blue with a white belly.
