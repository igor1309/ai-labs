Good morning, everyone, and thank you for joining the planning call for the community garden. Today we have three things to cover: the watering schedule, the tool shed, and the spring planting day. Please keep your questions until the end of each part.

First, the watering schedule. Last summer we watered every evening, and the beds near the fence still dried out in July. This year we will water early in the morning instead, before the sun is high. Each volunteer takes one week at a time. The sign-up sheet is on the board next to the gate, and there are twelve weeks still open. If you cannot come for a whole week, write your name in half of the box and we will find a partner for you.

Second, the tool shed. The door no longer closes properly, and two of the long rakes are missing. We have a quote from a local carpenter for a new door and a simple lock. The cost is about two hundred dollars, which fits in this year's budget. The lock will have a code instead of a key, so nobody has to wait for the person who holds the key. We will share the code by email, never on the notice board.

Third, the spring planting day. It will be on the second Saturday of April, from nine in the morning until one in the afternoon. We need people to bring gloves, small shovels, and water bottles. Children are welcome, and there will be a table where they can plant seeds in paper cups and take them home. The school has offered to lend us six folding tables and a tent, in case it rains.

A few smaller notes. The compost bins are full, so please do not add anything until we turn them next week. The water tap by the north corner leaks a little; it has been reported to the city. And thank you to everyone who brought spare seedlings last month. The tomatoes from the shared bed went to the food bank, about forty kilograms in total.

Now, about the budget for next year. We received a small grant from the neighbourhood association, and we would like to spend part of it on raised beds for people who cannot kneel. Two raised beds cost about the same as the new shed door. We will vote on this at the next meeting, so please think about it and tell us what you prefer.

Finally, the next meeting will be on the first Tuesday of next month, at seven in the evening, in the library meeting room. If you would like to add something to the agenda, send a short note to the coordinator by the Friday before. That is all for today. Thank you for your time, and see you in the garden.
