# Call text — QA

A Core, driven through fakes of its own interfaces: written tracks that
grow on cue, and per track a live text that turns each recorded word-tone
into a known word, can be held loading, and can fail. A word comes back
only if its audio was given, so every row reads back text that depends on
the recorded audio, before the recording ends.

## S-1 What I say is read as mine as I say it

A sentence written on the mic track one word at a time: each word comes
back as mine before the next is written. Evidence: Missing.

## S-2 What they say is read as theirs as they say it

A sentence written on the call track one word at a time: each word comes
back as theirs before the next is written. Evidence: Missing.

## S-3 Nothing is read before it is recorded

A word delivered to the fake source but not yet written does not come
back; once written, it does. Evidence: Missing.

## S-4 Live text says it is getting ready

With live text held loading, call text reads as getting ready. Evidence:
Missing.

## S-5 Live text catches up from the start

30 s of distinct words written while loading; on release, every one comes
back, in order, from the first. Evidence: Missing.

## S-6 Live text switched off keeps the text so far

After the switch, the earlier words stay and words written later do not
come back. Evidence: Missing.

## S-7 Live text switched on fills in the call

20 s of distinct words written with live text off; on the switch every one
comes back from the first, marked as written later. Evidence: Missing.

## S-8 Failing live text is told

Live text fails; call text reads the failure, and the text so far stays.
Evidence: Missing.
