Audelin

A quiet companion for your calls, entirely on your Mac.

A live, selectable transcript of Russian calls with English terms: «я» from the mic, «они» from the call. Select a term, press a hotkey, read a short Russian explanation.

  • Records detected calls automatically and stops when they end
  • Live transcript in a floating panel
  • After the call: remote speakers named, optional notes
  • Next: explain a selected term through bb
