# Review

Invoke the **code-review-discipline** dev-skill before reviewing a diff or someone
else's code, before writing down any fail/warning finding, and before turning
findings into an implementation plan.

It also binds on your own work: **self-review before declaring an implementation
complete is unprompted** — nothing will ask you to, so the trigger has to be your
own claim that the work is done.
