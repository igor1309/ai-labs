## Competitive note (2026-07-05)

- **His live format is nearly identical to ours (Program 2).** 2-hour workshop = 30-min lecture on typical mistakes + **40–60 min live re-doing a solved Jira task together** + 30-min Q&A. The "take a real, already-solved task and do it again live" move is exactly our Program 2 opener. He is already selling the demonstration format on-site to companies.
- **The testimonial names our opening.** Raiffeisen tech lead: the format "делаем задачу и походу спрашиваем" landed — **but "не хватило какой-то больше глубины" (lacked more depth).** That is the precise gap our Socratic structure targets: the layered questions, the honest "where it breaks," the *why* beneath the *how*. Depth is the differentiator against his format, confirmed by his own reference.

That buyer (enterprise-safe AI coding) stays underserved.
- **Channel signal:** he is doing paid on-site corporate workshops (B2B, in-person), not only the cohort course — a distribution lane distinct from recorded lectures.
