## How this feeds the base

Against the open questions in `decision-matrix.md` and `positioning-notes.md`:

- **"For an EN-market move, do we need a new survey of EN competitors before treating the slot as open?"** — Partially answered. EN market surveyed: awareness layer saturated ($0–49), premium hands-on thinner (Section, Every, AI Makerspace). The EN slot is real but not empty.
- **Format white space.** The **two-person Socratic / demonstration format is unoccupied in *both* markets** — nobody does expert + peer working a real task on camera. This is a HOW/differentiation lever, and it maps onto the still-open "async durable artifact-led format" slot.
- **CJ2 ("move from dabbling to shipping with AI as the default")** is strongly corroborated by mid-2026 demand: everyone is AI-aware and has dabbled; the live, unmet pain is **grounded work on their own specific real problems**, not awareness.
- **Anti-hype = trust** is a sharper lever in RU («развод», «много воды») than the base's sample alone showed — supports discipline/verification positioning over empowerment/speed.
- **Access friction (RU)** is real but is a **webinar topic, not lecture content**: Chinese models (DeepSeek/Qwen) and local (YandexGPT/GigaChat) cover the no-VPN path.
