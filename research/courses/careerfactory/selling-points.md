# CareerFactory Vibe-Coding Course Selling Points

## Core Selling Proposition

The course sells non-developers a fast path to building and deploying real applications with AI. Its central contrast is "become a real programmer in 8 weeks, not a year" — and "vibe coding for a healthy person," positioned between two rejected extremes: the oversimplified "just install Cursor" pitch and the gatekeeping "you must be a programmer" pitch.

## Primary Selling Points

### Speed to a Real Result

The headline promise compresses what is framed as a year of study into 8 weeks, with a working application within a month. The buyer gets a deployed product, not just knowledge.

### Built for Non-Developers

No formal IT education or experience is required. The offer explicitly targets people who lack IT knowledge but want to ship real products, removing the biggest perceived barrier.

### Real Tools, Not Toys

The course leans on professional tooling (Git, GitHub, Cursor, Docker, VPS) and real IT-company practices, distancing itself from app constructors and no-code "toys." This frames the outcome as production-ready and scalable.

### Concrete 8-Week Artifact

Every participant builds their own Telegram bot, evolving it from a first project to an AI-integrated, 24/7-deployed product. The tangible artifact gives the program a clear spine and a portfolio piece.

### "Vibe Coding of a Healthy Person" Method

A short, memorable, meme-flavored positioning that names the author's middle path between too-simple and too-hard. A nameable method packages the value.

### Independence and Cost Control

Teaches deploying on inexpensive VPS instead of subscription constructors that "drain money monthly," and frames the skill as freedom from dependence on developers and budgets.

### Security as a First-Class Topic

Digital hygiene — protecting API keys and passwords while using AI tools — is taught as part of the skill, addressing a real fear of working with AI on real systems.

### Career and Market Timing

The "vibe coding is the new Excel" section argues the skill is becoming a hiring requirement (AI tasks in interviews, AI-assistant-allowed coding interviews, startups writing 95% of code with AI, vacancies requiring vibe-coding experience). It frames the course as career leverage, with a "within 1-2 years it's on every second résumé" urgency.

### Live Cohort With Recordings

Two live webinars per week with Q&A, weekly practical homework, and recordings for review. The live anchor plus practice-every-week structure signals accountability and momentum.

### Founder Credibility

Глеб Михеев is presented as Ex-CPO of Skyeng, a serial entrepreneur, a self-described "100x developer" who replaces a whole team, and the author of a popular agentic-coding program.

### Tiered Pricing With a Wide Range

Four tariffs from 19,900 ₽ (watch-only) to 154,000 ₽ (one-on-one) let buyers self-select by budget and support level, with a 10% discount plus an additional promo-code 10%, installment options, and a stream-start deadline for urgency.

## Pain Points Addressed

### "I Have an Idea but No Money for a Prototype"

Response: build it yourself in a couple of weeks for a fraction of hiring developers (positioned against "from 500,000 ₽/month" with "90% take the money without a result").

### "My Skills Will Become Obsolete Because of AI"

Response: acquire the in-demand AI-building skill now and stay a step ahead of the market.

### "I Depend on Technical Specialists"

Response: control the process yourself, see results quickly, and stop being blocked by developers and budgets.

### "Code Scares Me"

Response: you do not write code by hand — the neural network does; you only need to be ready to look at it, with the LLM as your learning helper.

### "It's Too Hard / It's Too Simple"

Response: the "healthy person" middle path — real programming concepts without years of syntax study.

## Comparison Framing

The page anchors price against two alternatives the buyer already distrusts:

- Hiring developers — from 500,000 ₽/month, high risk of paying without a result, full dependence.
- Learning to program (Python/JS) — from 150,000 ₽/year, a long marathon, and "developers themselves are switching to neural networks."
- The course — from 19,900 ₽, build any product yourself in a couple of weeks.

## Buyer Personas

### Aspiring Founder

Has an idea, lacks a prototype budget. Best message: ship your own prototype this month instead of paying or waiting for developers.

### Career-Anxious Professional

Fears AI displacement. Best message: convert the threat into an in-demand, hireable skill.

### Frustrated Non-Technical Operator

Tired of depending on engineers. Best message: take control and see results in days.

## Objections and Responses

### "I Can't Program"

Response: no programming experience required; the neural network writes the code, you direct it.

### "It Won't Be Real / Production-Quality"

Response: the course uses professional tools and real IT-company practices and ends with a deployed product.

### "What If It Doesn't Work for Me?"

Response: pro-rated refund by services rendered; no result guarantee, but a guarantee of maximum effort plus permanent access to materials.

### "When Can I Start?"

Response: group tariffs start in June; watch-only and community tariffs grant instant access at any time.
