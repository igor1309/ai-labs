Agister — Name Brief

Core idea
Agister is an old institutional role: a custodian who manages entrusted animals, grazing rights, allocation, and associated accounting. The metaphor suggests coordination, stewardship, allocation, and governed autonomy.

Naming character
Institutional, slightly archaic, technical, understated. Familiar enough to pronounce, uncommon enough to feel ownable. The hidden Magister → Agister relationship adds a small discovery moment without defining the name.

Possible product territories
Suitable across related families rather than one fixed category:

* AI agents, orchestration, and runtimes
* AI infrastructure and control planes
* workflow and developer automation
* distributed execution systems
* resource scheduling, metering, and governance
* tool/MCP coordination layers
* autonomous-system supervision

Semantic territory
Stewardship over command. Coordination over control. Independent actors operating within managed shared resources.

Brand direction
Best suited to products that feel infrastructural, durable, composable, and quietly authoritative rather than conversational or anthropomorphic.
