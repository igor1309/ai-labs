# The investigator: draft, now recorded in ADRs

This note held the owner's decisions on Multica LAB-126 and LAB-127 and
the draft proposals that followed from
[`pre-implementation-investigation.md`](pre-implementation-investigation.md).
Two ADRs are now the record:

- [ADR-028](../../../agister/docs/adr/028-a-run-keeps-its-output.md): a
  run keeps its output's bytes, the cache holds a disposable copy, and a
  file param may name a run's output.
- [ADR-029](../../../agister/docs/adr/029-the-investigator-grounds-a-task.md):
  the investigator, the grounding, its two checks at the entry, and the
  brief as task and grounding.

The proposals on standing knowledge, what standing instruction files
hold, are in neither ADR: they are Multica LAB-134.
