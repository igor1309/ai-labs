# Cairana — Name Brief

## 1. Name

**Cairana**

Working pronunciation:

- English: **Kai-rah-na**
- Russian: **Кайрана**
- Alternative reading risk: **Kay-rah-na / Sai-rah-na** depending on market

## 2. Product Context

**Cairana** is a working name for an AI assistant for psychologists.

The product is not positioned as an autonomous therapist, diagnostic system, or clinical decision-maker.  
It is positioned as a professional assistant that helps psychologists with:

- structured session notes;
- case context;
- treatment continuity;
- follow-up preparation;
- between-session reflection;
- client-history memory;
- working drafts for clinical documentation.

## 3. Core Positioning

**Cairana helps psychologists turn sessions into structured notes, working context, and next steps.**

Alternative positioning lines:

- **Cairana is an AI assistant for psychologists: notes, context, and next steps after each session.**
- **Cairana helps psychologists document sessions, preserve case context, and prepare follow-ups.**
- **Cairana supports psychologists with structured notes, case memory, and continuity of care.**

## 4. Naming Rationale

Cairana works because it is:

- invented enough to avoid direct category clichés;
- readable in English and Russian;
- not overly soft, sweet, or wellness-coded;
- not medical/pharma-sounding;
- not built from obvious words like `psy`, `therapy`, `mind`, `care`, `note`, or `bot`;
- suitable for SaaS, assistant, or clinical workflow positioning.

The name has no heavy literal meaning. This is useful: the product meaning can be anchored through context instead of fighting existing semantic baggage.

## 5. Tone

Cairana sounds:

- calm;
- professional;
- slightly premium;
- structured;
- human-compatible;
- not aggressive;
- not infantilized;
- not decorative.

It sits between:

- a personal assistant name;
- a clinical workflow product;
- a quiet AI workspace.

## 6. What Cairana Is Not

Cairana should not be framed as:

- an AI therapist;
- a diagnostic assistant;
- an emotional chatbot;
- a mental-health companion for clients;
- a wellness or meditation app;
- a journaling app for consumers;
- a generic notes app.

The name works better when attached to professional practice, not consumer self-help.

## 11. Basic Hygiene Findings So Far

Initial lightweight check found no obvious direct collision in:

- AI assistant for psychologists;
- therapy notes;
- EHR;
- clinical documentation;
- mental-health automation;
- scam/fraud results by exact name.

Known soft concerns:

- possible relation to **Cairanne**, a French place name, with Occitan form **Cairana**;
- some wedding/event-style footprint;
- possible personal-name or low-volume miscellaneous usage;
- pronunciation ambiguity around `Cai-`.

These are not current blockers, but they require a deeper verification pass before adoption.
