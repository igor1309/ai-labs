Cairana

Keep the session inside your own clinical thinking.

A private practice workbench. It turns session residue into durable Session Records, maintains Case Memories, and projects across your Practice — instead of exporting each session as a note to bill.

Two spines

Client continuity and practitioner self-observation

Cairana is built on two backbones: holding the thread across a client's sessions, and turning your whole caseload into a mirror on your own practice.

Continuity

Session Record

Each session committed as a structured record — reviewed and trusted, not a raw transcript to reprocess.

Continuity

Case Memory

A maintained current view of the case, plus a journal of meaningful changes over time.

Continuity

Client Brief

A pre-session brief drawn from Case Memory, so you re-enter the case fast and oriented.
