Working name · Research stage

Cairana

Cairana helps psychologists turn sessions into structured notes, working context, and next steps. A professional assistant — not an autonomous therapist, diagnostic system, or clinical decision-maker.

Research-stage pain map

Visible pains in solo practice

These cards are exploration directions from pains visible in solo-practitioner work: post-session documentation, scattered case memory, treatment-thread drift, and data/accountability burden.

Notes

Session material becomes structured drafts for review and editing.

Context

Working case history stays accessible without reconstructing it from scattered notes.

Continuity

Previous sessions, current themes, treatment goals, and next steps stay connected.

Professional control

Cairana assists. The psychologist reviews, edits, signs, and remains responsible for the record.

Early-stage research page. Cairana is a working name, and these directions are still being validated against practitioner interviews, evidence strength, and naming checks.
