Agonara

An API-first structured learning program environment built around curated programs, progression, evidence of mastery, and agent-ready backend contracts.

From the ancient Greek agon — exercise, trial, disciplined striving. Curators assemble programs from trusted sources; learners move through them with clear sequence, checkpoints, and evidence of understanding. AI assists, but does not own the curriculum.

The core idea

Separate what is owned by whom

Agonara keeps the shared program corpus, personal learning state, participation, and operational authority in distinct ownership boundaries — so each can evolve independently without collapsing into one shared product-state blob.

CORPUS · vs · STATE

Program corpus is curator-owned

Topics, concepts, materials, checks, and criteria belong to the curator. A learner's answers never mutate them.

STATE · vs · CORPUS

Student state is learner-owned

Attempts, evaluations, progress, and weak areas belong to the learner and are read owner-only.

DRAFT · vs · ACCEPTED

LLM output is assistant material

Generated suggestions stay draft until a human curator explicitly accepts them into the corpus.

PARTICIPATION · vs · IDENTITY

Membership is separate from identity

Program participation lifecycle and policy are owned apart from identity, credentials, and Admin authority.

Architecture at a glance

Five module boundaries

The domain is split into five modules with explicit ownership. Other modules ask for effective participation decisions rather than reading each other's policy internals.

Corpus Storage

Accepted program corpus

The single home for content curators have explicitly accepted.

Owns: programs, topics, concepts, definitions, principles, accepted checks, criteria, material references, source metadata.
Program Membership

Participation lifecycle

Join, leave, rejoin, invitations, participant visibility, and effective-participation decisions.

Owns: membership status & policy, invitation lifecycle, exceptional recovery hooks.
Curator Workspace

Assemble & accept content

Research, draft, refine, and accept program content — including LLM-assisted suggestions and coverage-gap diagnostics.

Owns: sources, candidate topics/concepts/materials/checks, draft criteria, curator decisions.
Student Workspace

Personal learning state

Progression inside joined programs, with assessment execution and progress interpretation embedded.

Owns: answers, attempts, evaluation evidence, mastery, weak areas, learner-state interpretation.
Identity & Role Management

Identity & operational authority

The stable subject of ownership plus operational primitives.

Owns: internal identities, credential bindings, Admin role, role invariants, access recovery.
Cross-cutting

Operational concerns

Logging, observability, health/diagnostics, request correlation, and backup/restore cut across modules without owning their state.

Covers: audit reads, completion logs, health, diagnostics, full backup & restore.

Current implementation snapshot

What is implemented today

Backend, domain, and product surfaces shipped as vertical slices. Each slice carries storage, domain commands, session-backed HTTP routes, backup/restore, and — where noted — a /learn product UI.

Identity

Identity & Operations

First-Admin bootstrap, local-password login, server-side sessions, Admin identity ops, audit reads, health, diagnostics, request correlation, completion logs, backup & restore.

Membership

Program Shell & Membership

Program creation, Student self-join/leave/rejoin, invitation lifecycle, participant listing, Student removal, curator leave, Admin exceptional recovery/revocation, effective-participation checks, event reads.

Corpus

Corpus Curation

Program sources, corpus candidates, acceptance/rejection, accepted corpus revisions, corpus event reads, and backup/restore support.

Navigation

Topic / Concept / Material

Accepted topics, concepts, definitions, principles, material references, relationships, historical revisions, curator candidate routes, student-safe traversal, and a /learn navigation UI.

Checks

Accepted Checks & Criteria

Assessment criteria, open-answer checks, accepted revisions, and curator / student / admin read separation.

Student

Open-Answer Workspace

Student submission, idempotency, owner-only attempt detail/list reads, evaluation status/evidence reads, evaluator orchestration, worker execution, and backup/restore support.

Assessment

Quick Check

Session-backed routes, candidates, accepted revisions, protected answer keys, student-safe delivery projections, immutable attempts, matched evidence, and a /learn Curator/Student UI.

Student

Learner-State Interpretation

Explicit target-link curation metadata, read-time owner-only progress and weak-area summaries, supporting-evidence target detail, and a /learn learner-state UI.

Assessment

Readiness Checks

Topic- or named-period readiness definitions, evidence-backed explicit Student readiness attempts, idempotent retry, owner-only attempt reads, and a /learn readiness UI.

Curator

Coverage Gaps

Read-only Curator Workspace diagnostics over accepted corpus, deterministic draft_guidance reports, protected redaction, and a /learn filtering / drill-down UI.

Curator · LLM

LLM-Assisted Suggestions

Curator Workspace-owned deterministic-provider slice for redacted draft suggestions, origin inspection, accept/reject review, draft materialization without accepted-corpus promotion, and a /learn review UI.

Admin

Admin Board

A local operations board for bootstrap, sign in/out, Identity management, operational access inspection, audit inspection, and exceptional membership recovery/revocation.

Deferred: recommendation ranking and persisted mastery models still need separate specs; student- and curator-facing product UI beyond the shipped /learn slices is not yet implemented.
