# Agonara Brief

This brief gives the visual identity designer two things: what Agonara is, and
where its name comes from. It deliberately contains **no visual direction** — no
palette, type, mark concepts, references, or examples. Those decisions are the
designer's.

## The Product

Agonara is a learning program environment. Its center is the **program**: what
should be learned, in what order, by what evidence, and under whose curation. It
is not a note app, course directory, LMS, or AI tutor.

It is **bring-your-own-agent (BYOA)**: operators run their own private,
self-hosted instance and connect their own agent through its API. Curation stays
human-owned, and AI assists rather than authors — the agent operates the
product, not the curriculum.

What people do with it:

- **Curators** shape trusted sources into a structured program, connect concepts
  to materials, define checks, and accept what belongs.
- **Learners** move through the program with materials, concepts, checks, and
  feedback, keeping the thread instead of losing it.
- **Admins/operators** run the private instance: bootstrap it, manage identities
  and access, inspect audit events, and preserve program and learner state
  through backup and restore.

Progress stays grounded in evidence that belongs to the learner.

## Name Origin

The name **Agonara** draws from the ancient Greek root **agon**: contest,
struggle, exercise, trial, and disciplined striving. The emphasis is not
competition against others, but structured effort toward mastery — learning as a
disciplined passage through challenge.

The name is not self-explanatory: without supporting copy it can read as a
fantasy place name or an abstract brand.
