Architecture · Module Boundaries

How Agonara is put together

A five-module domain split with explicit ownership, a private self-hosted runtime, and ADR-backed boundaries. This is the deep companion to the project landing — boundaries here mirror the Module Responsibility Map and the accepted ADRs.

Domain decomposition

Five modules, explicit ownership

The learning domain is split so accepted corpus, curator drafts, student attempts, program membership, and identity/role state can each evolve independently. Every module lists what it owns and what it explicitly does not.

Corpus Storage

corpus.js · storage.js

Stores the accepted program corpus and the source materials programs are built from.

Owns

  • Programs, topics, concepts, definitions, principles
  • Accepted assessment items & criteria
  • Material references & source metadata
  • Accepted corpus revisions

Does not own

  • Curator drafts & LLM suggestions before acceptance
  • Student attempts & progress
  • Membership state

Key boundary: only accepted content lives here. Candidate material enters Corpus Storage only after explicit curator acceptance.

Program Membership

program.js

Manages participation of identities in programs.

Owns

  • Join / leave / rejoin behavior
  • Invitation lifecycle & admission policy
  • Participant visibility rules
  • Effective-participation decisions
  • Exceptional membership recovery hooks

Does not own

  • Identity lifecycle & credential bindings
  • Accepted corpus & curator decisions
  • Student answers, feedback, mastery

Key boundary: membership is product participation. Other modules ask for an effective participation decision instead of reading policy internals.

Curator Workspace

curator-workspace.js

Supports researching, assembling, refining, and accepting program content.

Owns

  • Research sources & candidate topics/concepts/materials/checks
  • Draft criteria & curator decisions
  • LLM-assisted suggestions (draft)
  • Coverage-gap diagnostics over accepted corpus

Does not own

  • Student learning history & progress
  • Identity lifecycle & membership policy
  • Accepted corpus (until acceptance)

Key boundary: curator work prepares corpus changes but is not accepted corpus until accepted. Curator participation is established through Program Membership.

Student Workspace

student-workspace.js

Supports personal learning state inside joined programs, with assessment execution and progress interpretation embedded.

Owns

  • Learning position, sessions, answers, attempts
  • Assessment feedback & evidence
  • Mastery, weak areas, review needs
  • Learner-state interpretation

Does not own

  • Accepted corpus & curator drafts
  • Membership lifecycle & policy
  • Material storage ownership

Key boundary: student activity reads accepted corpus after membership allows participation, but never mutates the corpus or owns membership lifecycle.

Identity & Role Management

identity.js

Defines and manages identities, credentials, operational roles, and access primitives.

Owns

  • Internal identities & credential bindings
  • Admin role, role vocabulary & invariants
  • Role-management operations & access recovery

Does not own

  • Program membership lifecycle
  • Program content & curator acceptance
  • Learner state & assessment logic

Key boundary: defines the internal subject and operational authority; it does not own program participation or workspace domain state.

Invariants

Separation rules that hold across modules

  • Accepted corpus and personal student state stay separate. Corpus changes never erase or overwrite existing attempts, progress, or history.
  • Program membership stays separate from identity, curator decisions, and learning state. Participation is neither learning state nor content work.
  • LLM output is draft material until a responsible module applies its acceptance rules. It never becomes accepted corpus or final progress state on its own.
  • Identity is the stable subject of ownership and attribution. Credentials and external accounts are bindings to an internal Identity, not the source of ownership.
  • Admin authority is instance-scoped and operational. It does not replace Program Curator ownership of accepted content.

Authority model

Roles & membership

Agonara separates identity, program-scoped product roles, the operational role, and credentials.

Product role

Student

Participates in a program and owns learner state within that program.

Product role

Program Curator

Curates a program and owns curator decisions within that program.

Operational role

Admin

Instance-scoped authority over identities, credential bindings, settings, and recovery-level operations. Not a learning role.

Narrative actor

Program Membership Manager

A policy actor for the Program Membership module — not a grantable role or authorization subject.

Product roles are program-scoped: ADR-004 assigns participation lifecycle and policy to Program Membership, ADR-005 accepts the first status and admission policy, and ADR-007 accepts the effective-participation decision contract.

Embedded behavior

Open-answer evaluation flow

Assessment execution lives inside Student Workspace. An open-answer attempt is recorded, orchestrated for evaluation, executed by a worker against accepted check and criterion revisions, and read back owner-only — the LLM never owns the resulting state.

Step 1
Submit
Student submits an open answer; the attempt is recorded idempotently against the accepted check.
→
Step 2
Orchestrate
Evaluator orchestration queues the attempt and tracks evaluation status.
→
Step 3
Worker
A worker executes evaluation against accepted criterion revisions behind a provider boundary.
→
Step 4
Evidence
Status and evidence are persisted as owner-only student state, never as corpus.

Governed by ADR-012 (attempt lifecycle), ADR-013 (evaluation governance), ADR-014 (orchestration), and ADR-015 (worker/provider boundary).

Runtime & storage

A small, recoverable backend

HTTP

Session-backed API

Node.js 22 API-first HTTP backend with server-side sessions, CSRF protection, and per-request correlation. Sessions are checked before CSRF on mutations.

Persistence

SQLite via better-sqlite3

Synchronous embedded SQLite holds all module state. No external database service is required for a private instance.

Operability

Health & diagnostics

/operations/health and /operations/diagnostics expose service and execution state; completion logs and audit reads support diagnosis.

Recovery

Backup & restore

Every slice contributes to backup export and restore validation, covering corpus, membership, workspaces, identity, and operational config.

Cross-cutting concerns

Concerns that span modules

These cut across modules and are designed without owning the product state of those modules.

Logging

Operational & domain events

Identity changes, membership changes, curator decisions, assessment attempts, LLM operations, backup/restore. Not product analytics or progress interpretation.

Observability

Health & execution state

Service health, failed jobs, LLM calls, evaluation, storage access, backup status. Not domain ownership or permissions.

Backup / Restore

Recoverability

All five modules plus material storage and operational config. Not curation or assessment logic.

Correlation

Request tracing

Per-request correlation threads through routes for diagnosis without leaking domain authority.

The v0.0.1 minimum operational contract is specified in Operational Cross-Cutting First Slice.

Deployment

Private self-hosted instance

Agonara targets a dockerized private self-hosted instance — normally a VPS or home server, with the same container running locally for development. It is API-first and bring-your-own-agent: an operator drives it with their own agent through the contract, while /learn, /admin, the operator CLI, and per-role operator skills stay first-class clients of the same backend contracts — none invent separate workflows or state. The default tenancy is a small private group.

Container

Docker image

agonara/Dockerfile builds the backend + UI image for self-hosted operation.

Compose

Persistent service

compose.yaml runs the service with persistent SQLite state.

Bootstrap

First Admin via CLI

A fresh instance becomes operational only after the first-Admin bootstrap establishes an active Admin identity.

The deployment shape is provisional and becomes a commitment only via ADR — see Deployment Target.
