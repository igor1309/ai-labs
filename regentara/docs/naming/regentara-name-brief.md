# Regentara

## Decision

**Regentara** is the selected name for the AI orchestrator product.

## Product fit

Regentara fits the product because the system is not positioned as the smartest agent in the loop. Its role is to govern, direct, route, constrain, and re-issue work across other agents, including stronger ones. That makes a governance-centered name more accurate than assistant, copilot, swarm, or orchestral naming.

The name aligns especially well with the product as:
- a harness over other agents
- a command and control layer
- a workflow governor
- a review-and-correction loop coordinator
- an orchestrator not limited to coding tasks

## Why this name won

Regentara has the right semantic center: governance, delegated authority, operational control.

It also avoids several bad naming traps:
- does not sound like a chat app
- does not sound like a coding copilot
- does not sound like a toy workflow builder
- does not sound like a generic enterprise scheduler
- does not rely on trendy “agent swarm” language

It is metaphorical enough to be brandable, but still legible enough to suggest control and orchestration.

## Tone

The name reads as:
- infra-serious
- controlled
- elegant
- directive
- not theatrical
- not overly aggressive

This is important because the product governs stronger executors, but is not meant to project cartoonish force or militaristic posture.

## Risk assessment

No decisive blocker surfaced in the naming review.

Observed considerations:
- scattered public-handle usage exists, but not at a level that blocks adoption
- the word exists as a Spanish verb form, but that is not inherently negative and is semantically compatible with the product’s role
- no clear blocker was identified in the visible pass for adjacent software/product usage

Practical conclusion: the name is viable and strong enough to adopt.

## Rejected nearby variants

These variants were explored but not selected:
- Regentis
- Regentor
- Regentorix
- Regentaris
- Regentoris
- Regentum
- Regentara-adjacent feminine and extended forms

Reasons for rejection included existing usage, weaker sound, lower elegance, excessive artificiality, or inferior fit relative to Regentara.

## Positioning note

Regentara should be presented as an orchestration and governance layer over agentic work, not as a general-purpose assistant. The product’s identity is strongest when framed around control, routing, review, and disciplined execution across multiple agents.
