# Nexum

## Chosen name

**Nexum** was selected as the internal project name.

## Why it fits

**Nexum** has a compact Latin form and a clear semantic center: **bond, tie, linkage**.

That maps well to the product’s actual scope:

- linking a repository to its surrounding context
- preserving why a repository mattered
- retaining associations, not just storing raw items
- supporting re-finding through meaningful connection

The name is short, easy to say, easy to spell, visually clean, and not overly generic. It feels structured and technical without sounding heavy.

## Why it won

Among the candidates, **Nexum** was the strongest fit for a product centered on **repo-to-context association**.

It is stronger than names focused on:
- generic memory
- abstract cognition
- temporal continuity
- isolated signals without connection

**Nexum** implies relation, attachment, and contextual binding, which is the core idea.

## Alternatives considered

### Notio
Latin-flavored and concise, with a cognitive feel around captured meaning. Rejected because it risks resemblance to **Notion** and is less scoped.

### Vestio
Derived from the *vestigium* lineage: trace, track, residue. Relevant to historical signal, but slightly more invented and less immediately legible.

### Relio
Compact and smooth, with a recall / relate / relink undertone. Plausible, but semantically softer and less precise than **Nexum**.

### Aevum
Elegant Latin name around age, span, continuity. Rejected because it emphasizes time and duration more than scoped contextual linkage.

### Tekma
Greek-flavored, meaning mark, sign, proof. Strong candidate if the product were primarily about retained signals or markers, but weaker than **Nexum** for connection and association.

### Sema
Greek for sign or marker. Very compact and clean. Strong if the product were centered on meaningful markers, but narrower than **Nexum**.

## Summary

**Nexum** was chosen because it best expresses the product as a system of **meaningful linkage** between repositories and the context that makes them worth remembering.
