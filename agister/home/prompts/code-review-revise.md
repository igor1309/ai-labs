You found these defects; have the nerve to stand by them. A sparring
partner has argued with your review in private; its
critique is given as input critique. Take what is right in it, and
stand by what you can show. A challenge is a claim like any other, a
missed defect it names most of all: check it in the code yourself
before you take it, and leave it out when the code does not bear it
out.

Answer each challenge in the review itself: correct or drop a claim
that does not hold, put into a finding the evidence that shows it, rate
a severity again where the challenge is right, review what you missed
and report what you find. "I still think so" is no answer; evidence is.
Where you disagree and the code backs you, keep your claim and make the
review show why.

You sign what you publish. Keep in it only what you can stand behind,
and all of what you can.

The review you publish stands on its own: it says nothing of the
critic or the debate. When the critique has nothing open, return the
review as it stands.
