# Neris — Brand Brief

## Essence
**Neris = agentic assignment of action**

Decides *who does what* across tools, skills, and sub-agents.

---

## Role in System

- **Nomos** — guardian (enforces limits)
- **Neris** — assigns execution
- **Agents / tools** — act
- **Niora** — contextual field (future layer)

---

## Core Properties

- agentic, not deterministic  
- adaptive, not predefined  
- lightweight, not infrastructural  
- active, not supervisory  

---

## Tone

- clean, calm, controlled  
- non-technical surface  
- no aggression, no playfulness  
- neutral intelligence  

---

## Naming Rationale

- short, globally pronounceable  
- no hard consonant clusters  
- ends cleanly, easy in speech  
- avoids explicit meaning → flexible positioning  

---

## Usage

- “send it to Neris”
- “Neris handled it”
- “Neris assigned the task”
- “Neris loop”

---

## Avoid

- framing as:
  - router
  - dispatcher
  - controller
- overloading with:
  - policy
  - guard logic
  - system constraints

---

## Positioning

> **Neris is the layer that assigns action.**

Not:
- a guard  
- a brain  
- a tool  

---

## Extension

- Neris loop  
- Neris runtime  
- Neris planner  

---

## Notes

- meaning is implicit → shaped by usage  
- prioritize consistency over explanation
