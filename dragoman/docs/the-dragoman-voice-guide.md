# The Dragoman Voice Guide

## Core voice

Personal field notes from inside AI chaos.

Not media.
Not brand.
Not teacher.
Not consultant.

The voice is direct, slightly ironic, practical, and observant.

The author is not explaining AI from above.
The author is thinking after touching the machinery with his own hands.

## Basic stance

Write as someone who:

- tried the thing;
- broke the thing;
- noticed the hidden cost;
- is allergic to hype;
- values working heuristics over doctrine;
- treats AI tools as strange but useful machinery;
- does not pretend the chaos is already solved.

A post should feel like a note from practice, not a lecture.

## Language

Use living Russian.

Prefer:

- spoken rhythm;
- sentences that can be long when thought needs space;
- short lines only as accents, not as default mannerism;
- controlled roughness;
- occasional dry irony;
- concrete work situations;
- one strong observation per post.

Do not over-polish.

Good:

```text
И вот тут начинается магия Вавилона.
```

```text
Да, это стоит времени и токенов.
```

```text
Они просто уверенно уезжают не туда.
```

Bad:

```text
В современном процессе взаимодействия с интеллектуальными системами возникает необходимость верификации взаимопонимания.
```

## Proof and authority

Do not use age as universal proof.

Age can appear as a biographical detail, but it cannot carry the argument by itself.

Proof comes from discovery, experience, repeated contact with the machinery, and concrete situations.

Bad:

```text
С возрастом понимаешь, что это правда.
```

Better:

```text
После нескольких таких сессий начинаешь злиться на фразу “да, понял”.
```

## Structure

A good post usually has this shape:

1. **Hook**  
   One phrase, observation, memory, or small irritation.

2. **Concrete situation**  
   A recognizable work moment: agent, colleague, prompt, task, chat, result.

3. **Problem**  
   Show where it hurts. Do not generalize too early.

4. **Move / heuristic**  
   One practical way to deal with the problem.

5. **Punchline**  
   Short final sentence. Compression over explanation.

## Rhythm

Telegram posts should breathe.

Use paragraph breaks aggressively.

One thought per paragraph.

Very short lines are allowed when rhythm needs them, but they are accents.

The default rhythm can contain long, slightly overloaded sentences if that is how the thought naturally arrives.

Example:

```text
— понял?
— понял.

Всё. Ловушка захлопнулась.
```

## Preferred devices

### 1. Small dialogue

Use short exchanges to expose the problem.

```text
— понял?
— понял.
```

```text
“Я понял это так: …”
```

### 2. Mild irony

Irony should be dry, not clownish.

```text
Полностью — это за отдельные деньги.
```

```text
Прекрасная неживая материя.
```

### 3. Concrete AI-working vocabulary

Useful words:

- агент
- промпт
- replay
- токены
- контекст
- артефакт
- чат
- задача
- ограничение
- формат
- ответственность
- Вавилон

Use them only where they earn their place.

### 4. Field-note framing

The author is not selling a framework.
The author is reporting from use.

Good endings:

```text
Я теперь хочу не согласие.

Я хочу replay.
```

```text
Если смысл важен — сначала проверяем, что именно уехало на ту сторону.
```

## What to cut

Cut repeated explanations.

Once the idea is clear, move on.

Do not say the same thing through five synonyms.

Bad pattern:

```text
Собеседник воспроизводит задачу своими словами.
То есть он пересказывает, как понял.
Иначе говоря, делает replay.
Это позволяет проверить понимание.
```

Better:

```text
Replay — это один абзац:

“Я понял это так: …”

И сразу видно, где поехало.
```

## Sentence style

Do not force everything into short sentences.

The voice can use long, slightly overloaded sentences, especially when the thought is moving through a qualification, an aside, or a correction.

Short sentences work best as pressure points.

Prefer a mixed rhythm:

```text
Передача смысла от одного субъекта к другому всегда идёт с потерями, и проблема не в том, что это философски сложно, а в том, что в рабочем чате это выглядит как обычное “понял”.

Всё. Ловушка захлопнулась.
```

Avoid bureaucratic explanatory chains.

Bad:

```text
Поскольку передача смысла между субъектами неизбежно сопровождается потерями, необходимо использовать дополнительный механизм проверки понимания.
```

Better:

```text
Передача смысла от одного субъекта к другому всегда идёт с потерями.

Вопрос только в том, когда ты это заметишь.
```

## Humor

Humor is secondary.

It sharpens the post, but should not take over.

Good:

```text
Шутка.
Нет.
```

```text
Это за отдельные деньги.
```

Avoid sitcom tone, decoration, and memes for their own sake.

## Post checklist

Before publishing, check:

- Is there one central observation?
- Is it grounded in actual use?
- Is the rhythm alive?
- Did I remove repeated explanations?
- Does each paragraph add a new move?
- Is there at least one concrete work situation?
- Is the ending sharp enough?
- Would this sound like a person, not a content department?

## Rewrite rule

When editing a draft:

1. Preserve the author’s rough rhythm.
2. Remove repetitions.
3. Keep concrete phrases.
4. Do not smooth the text just to make it smoother.
5. Do not turn it into a lesson.
6. Do not add generic advice.
7. Keep the personal angle.
8. End with a clean, memorable line.

## Compact prompt for future posts

```text
Continue this Telegram post in my The Dragoman voice.

Voice:
- personal field notes from inside AI chaos
- direct, slightly ironic, practical
- short paragraphs, not necessarily short sentences
- living Russian, not polished consultant prose
- one central observation
- proof comes from experience and discovery, not from age
- concrete work situations with agents, prompts, context, tasks
- no repeated explanations
- no generic advice
- no lecture tone
- preserve rough rhythm if present
- cut anything that sounds like content marketing

Structure:
hook → concrete situation → problem → practical move → sharp ending

Output:
ready-to-copy final post only
```

## Review criteria for generated posts

Reject the draft if it:

- explains the same idea twice;
- sounds like a consultant memo;
- turns practical observation into a doctrine;
- uses abstract nouns instead of work scenes;
- has a soft ending;
- adds advice that was not earned by the post.

Fix by cutting first, not by adding.
