# Notio

Latin-flavored; concise, cognitive, scoped around captured meaning.

---

Good name.

Why it works

* Compact
* Memorable
* Calm, product-like
* carries the sense of notion / notice / noted meaning without being too literal
* fits a capture system better than something grandiose or infra-sounding

Why it fits your project

This system is about:

* capturing something worth returning to
* preserving a trace of meaning
* turning raw input into a retained item

Notio aligns with that well. It feels closer to:

* note
* notion
* recognized signal
* retained meaning

That is a good semantic zone for this product.

Possible downside

The only real concern is that it is close in sound/association to Notion.
Not identical, but the proximity is obvious enough that some people may hear it that way.

Not necessarily fatal. Depends on your tolerance for that shadow.

My take

As a project name for this capture/inbox system: yes, strong candidate.

It is better than many names because it is:

* not overengineered
* not too mythological
* not too technical
* not too generic
