AI assistant for psychologists. Cairana helps psychologists turn sessions into structured notes, working context, and next steps. Mizan BLE

Pay in Mizan between two devices over Bluetooth LE. A proof of concept: one iOS app with two modes. The host on an iPad enters an amount and advertises it; the client on an iPhone finds it, shows the amount, and confirms or declines, and the host shows a mocked payment result. Later versions test how far iOS lets the payment go without an explicit handshake or an app in the foreground. Planning stage; no app code yet.

Swift iOS iPadOS Core Bluetooth
