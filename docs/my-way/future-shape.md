# Future Shape Of My Way

## Purpose

This note exists to make the current transition state explicit.

`docs/my-way/` is in active transition. Skills are being extracted from these notes because they are reusable knowledge building blocks. At the same time, the final shape and long-term role of this folder have not been decided yet.

The goal of this note is to prevent false certainty and future confusion. The lack of a final vision is intentional, not accidental.

## What is already decided

- Skills are being extracted as reusable units of procedural and declarative knowledge.
- These skills are intended to be used as building blocks across different contexts, documents, and workflows.
- The extraction is valuable even without a finalized information architecture for `docs/my-way/`.
- The transition is being tracked explicitly in [plans/transition-to-skills.md](plans/transition-to-skills.md).

## What is intentionally undecided

- Whether `docs/my-way/` remains an active folder long-term.
- Whether it becomes primarily a philosophical or rationale layer.
- Whether it is later absorbed into another vision or process document.
- Whether it is eventually archived as historical inspiration after the skills become the operational source of truth.

These questions are not blocked by the current skill extraction work. They will be decided later, based on real use.

## Current working stance

For now:

- skills are being treated as the operational building blocks
- `docs/my-way/` remains a live transition space
- final cleanup, archival, absorption, or reclassification is deferred

This is a deliberate temporary state.

## What should inform the later decision

The future role of `docs/my-way/` should be decided from evidence such as:

- whether the skills are sufficient on their own in real work
- whether the notes are still needed for operational guidance
- whether the folder still adds value as philosophy, rationale, or historical lineage
- whether another document or structure emerges as a clearer home for this material

## Summary

`docs/my-way/` currently serves two roles at once:

- source material being extracted into skills
- a temporary home for the broader approach while its final shape is still unknown

That ambiguity is real and currently accepted.
