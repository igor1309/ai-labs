# FolioTell 0.6.1

## What's New

- Playback controls now stay responsive while a document is being read aloud.
- Play and Pause stay in sync during speech startup, and closing a document
  no longer leaves an earlier reader session running.
- Voice selection no longer pauses playback to refresh the installed voice
  catalog.
