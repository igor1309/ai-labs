# FolioTell 0.6.0

## What's New

- Reader Settings now has its own screen, including whether FolioTell reads or
  skips document front matter.
- Voice selection has a dedicated control, while Playback controls keep speed
  and Start Over together for quicker listening adjustments.
- When a higher-quality Enhanced or Premium Apple voice is already installed,
  FolioTell confirms it once and then keeps the reader uncluttered.
