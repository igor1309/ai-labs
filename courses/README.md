# AI Course Launch — Research & Design

Working folder for launching two AI courses.

- **Cohorts:** (1) low/no-tech — non-technical professionals, SMB owners, creatives; (2) tech-not-yet-AI — competent developers not yet using AI in their workflow.
- **Markets:** Global / English + Russian / CIS.
- **Formats:** recorded Socratic-style lecture series (two practitioners on camera) + live webinars.

## Design principles (as of this session — subject to revision)

1. **Demonstration, not prescription.** The series shows *"look, this is how it could be done"* — not *"here's how you should work."* No lecturing.
2. **No performance, no acting.** The two on camera are practitioners, not actors. Cut staged emotion and staged ignorance — it reads as low-trust. The value is **real work actually getting done**, shown plainly. Genuine questions between competent peers are fine (that's the Socratic part); faked naïveté is not.
3. **Audience level is mid-2026, not early-2025.** Everyone is already AI-aware and has dabbled. The dead premise is "what is AI / first contact." The live, unmet pain is **grounded work on their own specific, messy, everyday problems** — the gap between *"I use ChatGPT"* and *"I get real work done on the exact things that eat my week."*
4. **Arc: grounded → repeatable → trusted-with-stakes** (see synopses below).
5. **Tool choice** (frontier vs Russian vs Chinese) is a legitimate on-camera question, but **access/VPN/payment logistics are a webinar topic, not lecture content.** Chinese models (DeepSeek, Qwen) are accessible from RU without VPN; local models (YandexGPT, GigaChat) cover the no-friction path.

## Session synopses — draft (non-tech)

| # | Working title | The real pain it hits |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Your problem, not a demo problem** | "It's 70% there on anything that *matters*, and I redo the hard 30% myself." Grounding it in your real context closes the gap. |
| 2 | **From a lucky chat to a workflow** | "I got a great result once and can't reproduce it." Turn a recurring weekly pain into a dependable process. |
| 3 | **Trusting it with real stakes** | "Fine for drafts, but I can't put it on the critical path." Where it quietly breaks and what's safe. |

Tech-cohort spine mirrors this (adoption, not career pivot; "делегирование без потери контроля"; Cursor→Copilot→agentic on-ramp).
